# Detooz App Installation Guide

> Follow these steps carefully to install the Detooz app on your Android device.

---

## Step 1: Disable Play Protect

Before installing, you need to temporarily disable Google Play Protect:

1. Open **Settings** on your phone
2. Search for **"Play Protect"**
3. Tap on **Google Play Protect**
4. Tap the **⚙️ Settings icon** (top right corner)
5. Toggle **OFF** → "Scan apps with Play Protect"

> [!NOTE]
> This is required because the app is not from the Play Store. You can re-enable Play Protect after installation.

---

## Step 2: Install the App

1. Open the **Detooz APK** file
2. Tap **Install** when prompted
3. **DO NOT open the app yet** — close the installer

---

## Step 3: Enable Restricted Settings

The app needs special permissions to monitor notifications for scam detection:

1. **Long-press** on the Detooz app icon
2. Tap **App Info** (ℹ️ icon)
3. Scroll down to find **"Restricted Settings"**
4. Toggle it **ON**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This step is essential — without it, the app cannot access notification permissions for real-time scam detection.

---

## Step 4: Open & Login

1. Open the **Detooz** app
2. Sign in with **Google** or create an account
3. Complete the **Permission Wizard** (SMS, Contacts, Notifications)
4. Start using the app!

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "App not installed" error | Uninstall any previous version first |
| Google Sign-In fails | Ensure you have internet connection |
| Permissions not appearing | Complete Step 3 (Restricted Settings) |
