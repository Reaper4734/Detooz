SLIDE 2 — The National Crisis

- Heading: Digital Scams in India
- 1.2+ Billion Mobile Users
- Massive surge in SMS, WhatsApp, and Telegram fraud
- The Tactic: Exploiting fear, urgency, and authority
- The Language Gap: Scams arrive in native regional languages
- Most Vulnerable: Elderly, rural, and non-tech-savvy populations
- Annual Loss: Estimated ₹10,000+ crore to digital fraud

---

SLIDE 3 — Local Ground Reality & The Human Cost

- Heading: Field Work: Survey & Case Study
- The Survey: Avoided generic questions; tested real identification
    - Result: High general awareness, but low ability to identify sophisticated scams
- The Case Study: Real local victim lost ₹10–12 lakhs
    - Victim Profile: Educated, generally aware of digital fraud
    - The Mechanism: Exploited extreme urgency and greed, bypassing logic entirely
- Takeaway: In the heat of the moment, human judgment fails

---

SLIDE 4 — The Core Insight

- Heading: The Psychology of a Scam
- Insight: A scam is a psychological attack, not just a technical trick.
- Awareness campaigns are necessary but insufficient
- Panicked users cannot rationally verify links in split seconds
- Requirement: A technological system that intercepts the threat before the user has to make a judgment call
- Proposed Solution: Detooz

---

SLIDE 5 — Introducing Detooz

- Heading: What is Detooz?
- An AI-powered, automated scam detection mobile application
- How It Works:
    - Runs silently in the background
    - Monitors incoming messages (SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram)
    - Provides instant notifications with plain-language explanations
- Core Principle: Active intervention with zero user effort

SLIDE 8 — The AI Strategy

- Heading: On-Device AI Model
- Proposed Model: MobileBERT (Lightweight & Mobile-optimized)
- Training Concept:
    - Utilizing 90,000+ real-world messages across multiple Indian languages
    - Validation Technique: Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation to ensure the model performs consistently across all scam categories without overfitting.
- Classification Strategy: Safe / OTP / Scam
- Target: High accuracy without relying on cloud servers
- Privacy Focus: 100% offline inference capability

SLIDE 10 — Protecting the Vulnerable

- Heading: The Guardian System
- Designed to protect non-tech-savvy relatives
- Mechanism: Link Detooz app to a parent or grandparent's phone
- The Alert: If the relative receives a suspicious message, the "Guardian" receives an instant notification
- Result: Enabling tech-savvy family members to provide remote protection
